Tide chart vs our forecast, tide by tide

The details sheet for one high tide, showing what islanders would conclude from the printed tide chart and the old 9.5 ft rule next to what our forecast says against the road’s measured height (9.9 ft). For each: the highest water and when, how far over (or under) the line it gets, and when the road is underwater. Numbers are from this morning’s live forecast. Only the top of the sheet changes; the “how our forecast differs” section and the report button stay as they are.
