A's readability, B's skimming

Four takes on keeping A's full, readable times while making it easy to skim for morning, afternoon or evening — the thing you liked about B. Parts of the day mean the same everywhere: overnight 12–6am, morning 6am–noon, afternoon noon–6pm, evening 6pm–12am. Past closures drop off. Each part of the day has a line icon (moon, sunrise, sun, sunset), and every day ends with a quiet line of NOAA's high-tide times and heights for boating.
