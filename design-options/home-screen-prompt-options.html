Prompting people to add the site to their home screen

iPhones don’t let a website open the “Add to Home Screen” prompt, so on an iPhone we can only explain the steps. Android can show a real “Add” button. In newer Safari the Share button is inside the ⋯ menu, so the steps say that. In A, B and D the note would only appear on a phone, on someone’s second visit, not once the site is already opened from the home screen, and never again after it’s closed. The wording is a first draft; the exact Safari steps get checked on a real iPhone before anything goes live.
